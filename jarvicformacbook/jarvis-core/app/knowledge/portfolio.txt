**End Client:** Emaar / Burj Khalifa
**Description:** Patent-pending smart-building intelligence platform delivered for the world's tallest building. Led the formal patent filing process covering IoT device architecture, blockchain data models, and AI inference pipelines.
**Tech Stack:** Next.js, TensorFlow Lite, MQTT, Hyperledger Fabric, GCP, Kubernetes, Python, YOLOv8, PostgreSQL.
**Key Achievements:** 35% projected energy reduction, Patent Filed.
